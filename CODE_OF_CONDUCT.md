# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple standard: be respectful, specific, and useful.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different experience levels, hardware setups, and benchmark goals.
- Focus critique on reproducibility, evidence, and maintainability.
- Be clear when a result is hardware-specific or not yet independently verified.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Misrepresenting benchmark results or hiding important setup details.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and issues that do not meet this standard. Serious or repeated violations can result in a ban from participating in the project.

To report a concern, open a private security advisory when appropriate or contact the maintainers through GitHub.
